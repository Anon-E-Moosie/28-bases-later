[h1]28 Bases Later — Claim & Manage Multiple Safehouses[/h1]
[b]Build 42 Multiplayer[/b]

Why settle for one safehouse when Knox County is full of empty houses?

[b]28 Bases Later[/b] lets you claim and manage multiple safehouses at the same time in multiplayer. Claim a main base, a gas station outpost, or a backup cabin in the woods—just claim them like normal.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Multiple Safehouses:[/b] Claim more than one base (default limit is 28, configurable by admins).
[*] [b]28BL Menu:[/b] Press [b]F7[/b] to open the 28BL menu and view all your claimed bases in a clean, resizable window.
[*] [b]Rename Bases:[/b] Give your bases custom names so you know which one is which.
[*] [b]Choose Your Respawn:[/b] Pick which base you want to respawn at after you die.
[*] [b]Release Bases:[/b] Easily release any base you don't need anymore.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How to Use[/h2]

[olist]
[*] Walk into any unclaimed building.
[*] Right-click inside and claim the safehouse like normal.
[*] Press [b]F7[/b] to open the 28BL menu.
[*] Select a base from the list to rename it, set it as your respawn location, or release it.
[/olist]
