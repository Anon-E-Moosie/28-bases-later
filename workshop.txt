[h1]28 Bases Later — Claim & Manage Multiple Safehouses[/h1]
[b]Build 42 Multiplayer[/b]

Why settle for one safehouse when Knox County is full of empty houses?

[b]28 Bases Later[/b] lets you claim and manage multiple safehouses at the same time in singleplayer and multiplayer. Claim a main base, a gas station outpost, or a backup cabin in the woods—just claim them like normal.

[hr][/hr]
[h2]Features[/h2]

[list]
[*] [b]Multiple Safehouses:[/b] Claim more than one base (default limit is 28, configurable by admins).
[*] [b]Basic Loot Tracking:[/b] Easily track what loot you already have stored at certain bases while exploring Knox County.
[*] [b]28BL Menu:[/b] Press [b]F7[/b] to open the 28BL menu and view all your claimed bases in a clean, resizable window.
[*] [b]Rename Bases:[/b] Give your bases custom names so you know which one is which.
[*] [b]Choose Your Respawn:[/b] Pick which base you want to respawn at after you die.
[*] [b]Release Bases:[/b] Easily release any base you don't need anymore.
[/list]

[hr][/hr]
[h2]How to Use[/h2]

[olist]
[*] Walk into any unclaimed building.
[*] Right-click inside and claim the safehouse like normal.
[*] Press [b]F7[/b] to open the 28BL menu.
[*] Select a base from the list to rename it, set it as your respawn location, or release it.
[/olist]

[h2]Changelog[/h2]
[b]v1.2.0[/b]
[list]
[*] Persistent Loot Tracking across Reconnects: Safehouse inventory item counts are now saved to local disk cache with debounced writing. Tooltips immediately remember all stored loot upon reconnecting without needing to re-visit or enter distant bases.
[*] Full RV Interior & Vehicle Compatibility: Full support for RV Interior and vehicle pocket dimensions. Teleportation into and out of RV interiors is automatically detected, scanning loot before chunks unload.
[*] Death Safehouse Protection & Self-Healing: Claimed safehouses are no longer lost when a player dies in multiplayer. Authoritative server storage self-heals missing safehouses back into the game engine across character lifecycles and server restarts.
[*] Safe RV Respawn & Chunk Streaming: Respawning at an RV interior safehouse now safely streams map chunks before positioning the character, preventing falling into the void and preserving vehicle exit interactions.
[*] Client Safehouse Engine Sync: Reconnecting clients immediately synchronize safehouse boundaries into local memory, preventing false "Claim Safehouse" context menu options on already-owned bases.
[/list]

[b]v1.1.0[/b]
[list]
[*] Added safehouse co-ownership and member sharing: invite online players (+ Add Member), evict members (- Kick Member), or leave shared bases (Leave Base).
[*] Redesigned the F7 dashboard into a resizable two-column layout with an interactive member roster and instant UI updates.
[*] Independent personal respawns: members and owners can each designate their own respawn safehouse.
[*] Safehouse loot tracking now includes items stored across all your co-owned and shared bases.
[*] Added admin claim restrictions in Sandbox Options: max building dimensions, non-residential claim toggles, spawn point protection, and days-survived requirements.
[/list]
